Common questions

Before you reach out.

How much time do I need each week?

For the audit call, just 60 minutes once. On the monthly plans, around 2–4 hours a week for filming. We handle the thinking, scripting and direction. On Creator OS it's mostly the two shoot days.

What if I'm not comfortable on camera yet?

That's easier to work with than someone with bad habits. A lot of our work is removing the overthinking that makes people stiff on camera. We build around the way you naturally talk, not a performance.

Do I need a big following to start?

No. We've grown pages from under 1,000 followers. It works whether you're starting from zero or stuck at 10k. What matters is a clear angle, not your current count.

Which niches do you work with?

Fashion, fitness, clothing brands, coaching, personal brands, business and entertainment. The system carries across niches. What changes is the angle and the audience, and we work those out together.

Do you guarantee followers?

No. Nobody honest can. We guarantee the work: a clear angle, strong scripts, and decisions based on your data. Our own pages and client results show where that usually leads.

What's the difference between the call and a monthly plan?

The call gives you a clear diagnosis: what's wrong, what to fix and what to do next, and then you run with it. On a monthly plan we're in it with you every week, writing scripts, reviewing hooks and building the system.

Can I start with the call and upgrade later?

Yes. The call is the best place to start if you're unsure. If a monthly plan makes sense after it, we'll tell you. If it doesn't, we'll tell you that too.
