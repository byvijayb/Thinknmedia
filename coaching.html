Common questions

Before you reach out.

still unsure? that's what the 1:1 call is for.
How much time do I need each week?

For the call, just 60 minutes once. On monthly plans, roughly 2–4 hours a week for filming. I handle the thinking, scripting and direction. You show up and record.

What if I'm not comfortable on camera yet?

That's easier to work with than someone with bad habits. A lot of my work is removing the overthinking that makes people stiff on camera. We build around your natural way of talking, not a performance.

Do I need a large following to start?

No. I've grown pages from under 1,000 followers. It works whether you're starting from scratch or stuck at 10k. What matters is a clear angle, not your current count.

What niches do you work with?

Fashion, fitness, clothing brands, coaching, personal brands, business and entertainment. The system carries across niches. What changes is the angle and the audience, and we figure those out together.

Can you guarantee followers?

No, and nobody honest can. I guarantee the work: a clear angle, strong scripts and decisions made from your data. My pages and Sanju's 30 days show where that usually leads.

What's the difference between the call and a monthly plan?

The call gives you a clear diagnosis: what's wrong, what to fix, what to do next. You take it and run. Monthly plans mean I'm actively involved every week: writing scripts, reviewing hooks and building the system with you.
