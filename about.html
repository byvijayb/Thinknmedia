Chapter 1 · The honest part

I failed 10th. Then I failed Inter.

Both times, money was the real reason. For the final exams we hadn't paid the fee. We went and asked, and somehow got a hall ticket. I retried and passed.

But the certificate is still sitting in that college office. We never had the ₹3,000 to collect it. So, no degree. Not for lack of trying. Even collecting your own results costs money, and we didn't have it.

Chapter 2 · Four years, not one rupee

0 views. 3 views. 7 on a good week.

I just kept uploading whatever felt right that day. I paid a content coach once. He told me things I already knew, in a more confident voice. I tried every "grow fast" trick I could find. I even bought fake subscribers once, out of desperation, and watched a number go up that meant nothing.

Four years. Not a single rupee came in.

That's exactly why Thinkn Media will never sell you shortcuts. I've tried them all. None of them work.

Chapter 3 · What actually changed

It wasn't posting more. It wasn't working harder.

It was one thing: understanding why someone watches a video till the end, and deciding that on purpose, before filming.

That's it. Angle first, then the hook, then the script, then the data.

₹17 lakhs in the 9 months after that.

Free advice

Here's how I'd think about your situation.

Not a course. Just what I'd actually do. Pick the one that sounds like you.

Don't think about growing yet. Just post something that's actually yours. Mine was IKEA bags into trousers. It was weird. That was the point.
Most likely you're posting things that someone else already said better. I did that for months. The fix wasn't trying harder. It was stopping the copying.
That one hit worked for a reason. Find it. Not the topic: the structure, the hook, the feeling it created. That part is repeatable. Everything else was noise.
I started with exactly ₹20,000. The trick wasn't the money. It was documenting everything, publicly, from day one.
